About Us

Who We Are

Founded: 2022 by two fashion graduates

Urban Textiles was started to support Durban’s creative community with quality materials and skills.

Our Mission

To provide quality fabrics, modern sewing patterns, and accessible sewing education to everyone in Durban.

Our Vision

To become Durban’s leading creative textile hub by 2028.

What We Offer

We sell fabrics, patterns and sewing accessories. We also run beginner and moderate sewing classes 6 days a week. All materials are included for your first class.
